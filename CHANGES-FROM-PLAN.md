# Changes from the development plan

Every place where the build differs from `development-plan-ticketing-systemv1.md` (draft 3), or where the plan left
something open and a choice had to be made to keep moving. Grouped by phase, newest last. Each entry says **what**
changed, **why**, and **where** to look. Decisions that need your answer are also listed in `DECISIONS.md`.

Legend: **Change** = differs from the plan. **Choice** = the plan was silent or ambiguous; this is what was built.
**Env** = local environment only, not the product.

## Phase 3: SLA policies and timers

| # | Type | What | Why | Where |
|---|------|------|-----|-------|
| P3-1 | Choice | **One shared SLA clock per ticket.** All of a ticket's timers measure the same thing — working time on the policy calendar since the SLA start, minus pauses — and each compares it with its own cumulative target plus extensions. Stored per timer: `seconds_used`, `running_since`, `due_at`, `extension_minutes`, `state` (RUNNING, PAUSED, STOPPED, SKIPPED), `breached_at`, `stopped_at`, `paused_reason`, `is_current`, `cycle`. MET/BREACHED are derived from STOPPED + `breached_at`. | D29 cumulative targets; makes pauses, priority change and re-arm consistent. | `services/sla.py` |
| P3-2 | Choice | A step stopped **after** its due time is BREACHED, with `breached_at` = the due time, even if the worker had not yet run. Pausing a step that is already past due records the breach the same way. | F6.6 "BREACHED when the due time passes while running". | `sla._check_overdue_now` |
| P3-3 | Choice | **Release re-arms** steps whose stop status is no longer reached; a re-armed step continues the shared clock (the time it was stopped counts, because targets run from the SLA start). If it is already past its target, it breaches at the next worker cycle with `breached_at` = the re-arm time. | F6.8 does not say how much time a re-armed step has. | `sla.on_transition` |
| P3-4 | Choice | **Reopen** archives the old timers (`is_current=false`) and starts a new SLA with the policy that matches the ticket's *current* priority and type. | F6.8 "reopen restarts all". | `sla.start` |
| P3-5 | Choice | **Priority change** (D28): new timers under the new priority's policy, each starting with the shared clock; a step MET before stays MET (matched by position); a step whose stop status the ticket has already passed is stopped at once (BREACHED if the clock is past its target); no policy for the new priority → the ticket has no SLA from then on. | D28 details. | `sla.on_priority_change` |
| P3-6 | Choice | Step stop statuses are limited to ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS and RESOLVED; the last step must stop at RESOLVED (S26). | The working path; NEW never stops a step, CLOSED is after RESOLVED. | `sla.validate_steps` |
| P3-7 | Change | `PUT /admin/sla-policies/{id}/metrics` matches steps by **position**: existing rows are updated, removed positions are deactivated, so escalation rules (Phase 4) stay attached to their step. Running timers keep their copied targets (F6.10). | Rules hang off `sla_metric`. | `sla.put_metrics` |
| P3-8 | Change | `PATCH /admin/sla-policies/{id}` changes name and active only; priority, type and calendar are locked (create a new policy instead). | A11.5 lists only name and is_active as changeable. | `sla.update_policy` |
| P3-9 | Change | `GET /admin/sla-policies?missing=true` lists priority × type combinations in the caller's scope that have neither a type policy nor a default policy. | S25 "a filter shows combinations … that have no policy". Not a new endpoint, a query flag on API-092. | `api/routes_sla.py` |
| P3-10 | Choice | `team_time_zone` on timers is the time zone of the **SLA policy calendar** (teams carry no calendar, D22/D23). | Name kept for the contract. | `sla.timer_view` |
| P3-11 | Change | The timer object also carries `minutes_target`, `minutes_extended`, `percent_used`, `stopped_at`; API-088 adds `has_policy`, `manual_pause`, `manual_pause_reason`, `time_zone`. | S06 SLA strip and S09 needs. | `sla.read_timers` |
| P3-12 | Choice | An extension never clears a breach that already happened (`breached_at` is set once). | F6.6. | `sla.manual_action` |
| P3-13 | Choice | Project and org admins list only type policies in their scope; default (platform-wide) policies are listed and edited by platform admins. A ticket with no matching policy uses the default calendar for its resolution time. | A8 G12 "Who". | `sla.list_policies`, `sla.start` |
